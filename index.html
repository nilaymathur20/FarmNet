How FarmNet Works

Simple steps to connect and transport your agricultural produce

1

Register Your Profile

Create your farmer or transporter account with detailed information about your services or requirements.

2

Post Your Requirements

Farmers post their transportation needs, including produce type, quantity, pickup location, and destination.

3

Get Connected

Our platform matches farmers with suitable transporters based on location, capacity, and requirements.

4

Negotiate & Confirm

Communicate directly, negotiate rates, and confirm booking details through our secure platform.

5

Track & Deliver

Monitor your shipment in real-time and ensure timely delivery with GPS tracking and updates.
